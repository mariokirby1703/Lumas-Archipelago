# Marbles! Balance Challenge Archipelago World

Marbles! Balance Challenge is the PAL version of Marble Saga: Kororinpa. This Archipelago world targets the PAL
RK6P18 release and uses an external Dolphin client.

## Goals

- Normal Stump Temple Level 10
- Hard Stump Temple Level 10

## Locations

- Level goals
- Stump Temple Pieces
- Green Gems
- Anthonys
- Trophies
- Tutorial levels
- Wii Balance Board levels
- Bonus world level goals

## Items

- World unlocks per difficulty
- Bonus level unlocks
- Hard Mode unlock
- AP-side Stump Temple Piece counter items
- AP-side Green Gem counter items
- Marble unlocks
- Figure Roller Head unlocks
- Vehicle unlocks
- Junk filler items
- Trap items

## Notes

This is an early release of the Marbles! Balance Challenge APWorld.

Green Gems and Stump Temple Pieces can be used as AP-side counter items even when their matching sanity option is off.
The `extra_counter_item_percentage` option controls how many extra counter items are added above the configured
requirement.

Crystal Sanity and Junk Factory content will be added in later updates.
